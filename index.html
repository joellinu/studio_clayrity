Our story

From a lump of clay to your morning cup.

Every Studio Clayrity piece takes five steps and about ten days to make. Scroll to follow one cup from the wheel to the kiln.

  1. 01

    Centre the clay

    Local stoneware is wedged by hand to push out air, then centred on the wheel until it spins perfectly true.

    Day 1
  2. 02

    Throw the form

    The walls are pulled up in three or four passes. The rings you feel on a tumbler are the potter's fingertips.

    Day 1
  3. 03

    Dry and trim

    Cups rest on wooden shelves until leather-hard, then go back on the wheel upside down to carve a clean foot.

    Days 2–5
  4. 04

    Dip in glaze

    Each piece is dipped in two or three glazes. Where they overlap, they will run, pool and break in the heat.

    Day 7
  5. 05

    Fire the kiln

    A slow climb to about 1,240°C, then two days of cooling. Opening the kiln is the moment every glaze reveals itself.

    Days 8–10
